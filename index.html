MODERN RECONSTRUCTION

Each letter becomes five stitches, one column per letter, read top to bottom. An empty cell is a knit stitch (0), a dot is a purl (1). The letter L is 01011, value 11.

02 / Every story carries its evidence

Secret-message stories drift from "possible" to "everyone knows" quickly. Each history page will show how strong its evidence is.

  • STRONG RECONSTRUCTION Norway, 1942. Red knitted caps became a resistance symbol and police banned them from 26 February 1942. [S01]
  • NEEDS A PAGE-LEVEL SOURCE Belgium, WWI. Railway watching by intelligence networks is well supported; the knitter dropping stitches for passing trains is not yet traced to a source. [S03]
  • DISPUTED Underground Railroad quilt codes. A story that matters culturally, without contemporary documentation of a standard code. [S14]
