# Capture guide
Keep originals and capture order. Drop material into this project's 00_INBOX with the swatch ID.

## Knitting
Photograph after each completed row, right side facing the camera, cast-on edge at the bottom.
Keep camera, background and lighting steady if practical; include needle and working yarn.
Record the true row count, including gaps. Do not duplicate images to fill gaps.
A cast-on frame has completedRows: 0; note bind-off separately.
Record gauge per a named distance rather than bare numbers.

## Frogging SW01
Capture both sides and close-ups first, and preserve a hand trace if possible.
Photograph after removed rows, or take short clips for later sampling.
completedRows means rows still in the fabric and decreases through the sequence.
Keep orientation steady. side records the face the camera sees, not the face just worked.
Record partial-row removal in note.

## Manifest
Put files next to manifest.json or in a relative frames/ folder.
Each frame needs file, alt, completedRows and side (RS or WS).
Empty frames means material has not arrived.
Example only:
~~~json
{"file":"frames/row-001.jpg","alt":"Right side with one completed row, cast-on at bottom","completedRows":1,"side":"RS","note":"After row 1"}
~~~
Keep message text out of alt text when visitors are invited to decode.
Do not label generated intermediate frames as photographs.
