A museum of textiles as communication, a lab that turns a message into a knittable pattern and back, and The Purloined Parcel, a small decoding game.

## Iterations

- **0.1** (2026-09-26): repo, stack, five-bit alphabet (T01), landing page.
- **0.2** (2026-09-26): error control layer (T02, T02b): framing, parity or Hamming codes, separators, checksum, slip detection.
- **0.3** (2026-09-26): the lab. Morse and Bacon (T03, T04), logical grid with orientation marker (T05), flat and round construction (T06), purl relief and two-colour carriers (T07), written rows, SVG chart and project files (T08), encoder and manual decoder pages (T09, T10), then row and stitch error reports, arrow keys, and PNG, PDF, XLSX and CSV exports (T09b).
- **0.4** (2026-09-26): the game and the archive. The Purloined Parcel with a drawn-fabric renderer, seeded cases and six levels in a folio of dossiers (G1 to G4). The archive with draft exhibits, the evidence template and a source register (T11 started, T12 drafted), exhibits written as Markdown files. Classical ciphers in the lab and game (T13). Structure errors pointed at their cells.
